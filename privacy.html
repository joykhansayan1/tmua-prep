Privacy Policy

TMUA Prep · Last updated: 21 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TMUA Prep (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no third-party analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

2. Data stored on your device

Study progress (answers, streak, bookmarks, mock history, topic mastery, preferences, test date) is stored only on your device using Apple on-device storage. It never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the App.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We never see or receive payment card details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you enable the optional daily study reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. This is handled by iOS on-device and is not sent to us or any third party. Disable anytime in the App or in iOS Settings.

5. No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites and do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). The App does not require Camera, Photos, Contacts, Location, Microphone, or Health access.

6. Children

The App is an education study aid for students preparing for university admissions-style maths tests. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Apple Account holders manage purchases.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: dmjks120@gmail.com.

This App is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to UAT-UK, Imperial College London, the University of Cambridge, LSE, Warwick, Durham, or any university admissions office.
